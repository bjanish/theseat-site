The Seat — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Overview

The Seat is a party game that runs entirely on your device and connects nearby phones directly to each other. We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit your personal information to any server or third party.

No Accounts

The Seat does not require an account, sign-in, email address, or phone number. The only information you provide is a display name, which is stored locally on your device and shared only with the other nearby players you choose to connect with during a session.

How Connections Work

The Seat uses your device's local network and peer-to-peer wireless features to discover and connect with nearby players. This connection is direct between devices in the same room. No game data travels over the internet, and no data passes through our servers — there are no servers.

What Is Shared During a Session

  • Your chosen display name is visible to other players you connect with.
  • Questions you submit are sent anonymously to the host's device. The host does not see who submitted which question.
  • Questions and session activity exist only for the duration of the session. When the session ends, this information is not saved by the app.

What We Do Not Collect

  • No names, questions, or session content are transmitted to us or any third party.
  • No location data.
  • No contacts, photos, or device identifiers for advertising.
  • No analytics or tracking inside the app.

Data Stored on Your Device

The Seat stores a small amount of information locally on your device, such as your display name and app preferences. This information stays on your device and can be removed by deleting the app.

Local Network Permission

The Seat asks for permission to use your local network. This is required so your phone can find and connect to other nearby players. It is used only for gameplay and not for any other purpose.

Children's Privacy

The Seat does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the app collects no personal data and requires no account, no personal information is gathered from users of any age.

Purchases

The Seat may offer a one-time in-app purchase to unlock unlimited hosting. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
